# FinTech, Fraud Prevention & AML — IT Operations Notes

Notes and reference material on fraud prevention, anti-money laundering compliance, and regulatory technology — written from an IT operations perspective.

I studied Financial Technology at MSc level at National College of Ireland (2:1, 2024), with dedicated modules on Regulatory Technology, AML compliance frameworks, and financial data analytics. These notes are a practical distillation of what IT professionals working in or around FinTech companies need to understand about the regulatory and fraud prevention environment they are supporting.

This repo exists because most IT helpdesk people have no background in financial compliance. When you support engineers, compliance officers, and fraud analysts at a FinTech company, understanding what they are building and why it matters makes you a much better IT support partner.
